Egyptian Cheque Management

Professional cheque handling for Egypt, built entirely on top of the standard Odoo account.payment and Bank Reconciliation flow. No parallel accounting, no core modifications.

Highlights

  • One Cheque = One Payment — every cheque is a real account.payment, preserving reconciliation, partial payments, audit trail and bank matching.
  • Incoming Cheques — cheques received from customers are parked on a configurable Incoming Cheques Account (Cheques Under Collection). The bank is not touched until collection.
  • Outgoing Cheques — cheques issued to vendors are parked on a configurable Outgoing Cheques Account (Issued Cheques). The bank is not debited at issue time.
  • Standard Outstanding Accounts — the cheque accounts are wired through the standard account.payment.method.line.payment_account_id, exactly like any other payment method.
  • Due Date Tracking — the cheque due date drives the journal item maturity date. Maturity status (Not Due / Due Today / Overdue) with filters for due today, next 7/30 days and overdue cheques.
  • Bank Reconciliation Native — a cheque is only Cleared when the standard bank reconciliation matches it (Dr Bank / Cr Cheques for incoming, Dr Cheques / Cr Bank for outgoing). No fake "Mark Cleared" button.
  • Mark as Deposited — operational tracking of the deposit date and bank journal for incoming cheques, with no accounting impact.
  • Mark as Bounced — accounting-sound bounce for unmatched cheques using the standard cancellation: reconciliations are removed and the partner balance is restored. Already-matched cheques are protected.
  • Duplicate Warning — warns on cheque numbers already registered for the same bank and payment type, with a direct link.
  • Multi Company — per-company cheque accounts and payment method lines; strict company checks everywhere.
  • Multi Currency — the cheque amount is always the payment amount; Odoo handles currency conversion.

How it works

  1. Set the Incoming/Outgoing Cheques Accounts in Accounting → Configuration → Settings → Cheque Management.
  2. Bank journals automatically receive the Cheque - Incoming and Cheque - Outgoing payment methods.
  3. Register a payment on an invoice and pick the cheque method: a Cheque Details section asks for the number, bank, dates and drawer.
  4. Follow the cheque from Accounting → Cheques (Received / Issued) and clear it through your normal bank reconciliation.
